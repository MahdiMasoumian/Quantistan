Trading refers to the process of buying and selling financial assets with the goal of profiting from price movements. These assets may include stocks, currencies, commodities, or other financial instruments. In its simplest form, a trader aims to buy an asset at a lower price and sell it at a higher price, or profit from a decline in its price.

Trading is not only a momentary action but a decision-making process in which an individual or system determines the timing of entry and exit based on available market information. This decision-making can be entirely human and experience-based, or it can be systematic and algorithmic, driven by data and predefined rules.

In modern financial markets, trading is often supported by analytical tools, historical data, and mathematical models, which help reduce emotional bias and make decisions measurable and testable. As a result, trading has evolved from a simple individual activity into a specialized field driven by data and technology.
