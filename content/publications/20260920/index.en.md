## **Proof-of-Concept Risk Assessment of Trading Algorithm Performance with Quantum Amplitude Estimation, Focused on Maximum Drawdown**

**Author:** Mohammad Mahdi Masoumian (Babol Noshirvani University of Technology)

**Publisher/Release date:** Posted: 20 Oct 2026 | Date Written: October 19, 2026

[**Download PDF**](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7635403)

**Abstract**

Maximum drawdown is among the most critical risk metrics, since other strategy deficiencies ultimately translate into capital loss and manifest as drawdown. Using drawdown as an illustrative case, this study presents an experimental proof of concept of quantum computing for risk assessment of trading algorithm performance. The probability that maximum drawdown exceeds 5% over eight trades (win rate 0.4054, reward-to-risk 1.92, 2% risk per trade) is estimated. Maximum likelihood amplitude estimation with a 15-qubit circuit on a noiseless Qiskit Aer simulator yields 59.257%, versus 59.324% by exact enumeration (absolute error 0.067 percentage points) and 59.310% by 200,000-path Monte Carlo. The code for this paper is available on GitHub.
