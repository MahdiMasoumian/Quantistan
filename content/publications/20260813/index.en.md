## **A Hybrid Machine Learning and Rule-Based Decision Support System for Short-Term Trading: A Case Study on XAUUSD**

**Author:** Mohammad Mahdi Masoumian (Babol Noshirvani University of Technology), Rasoul Shafaei (K. N. Toosi University of Technology)

**Publisher/Release date:** Posted: 17 Aug 2026 | Date Written: August 13, 2026

[**Download PDF**](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7279718)

**Abstract**

Financial markets, characterized by high liquidity and rapid capital flows, require reliable predictive tools to support trading decisions and risk management. This study evaluates several machine learning algorithms to identify a robust short-term price forecasting model and, based on these findings, develops a rule-based decision-support system for algorithmic trading. Using XGBoost with tailored feature engineering, the proposed model forecasts five-minute gold (XAU/USD) price movements over a one-hour horizon, achieving a root mean square error (RMSE) of 3.49 and a mean absolute percentage error (MAPE) of 0.11%. When operationalized as a complete trading strategy governed by a fixed-dollar-risk position-sizing rule, the system produced a cumulative net return of 130.62% ($13,062.38 profit on a $10,000 initial capital) over the January 2021-October 2025 period, with Monte Carlo simulation indicating a mean annualized return of approximately 30.7% and an 89% probability of annual profitability. These results highlight the model's effectiveness for data-driven investment and automated trading applications.
